How it works

Simple, safe, and Algerian.

Four steps to get the job done — with trust built into every one.

1

1. Search & filter

Pick a service category, choose a wilaya, or use your GPS to find pros within 2–25 km. Guest browsing is free — no account required.

2

2. Chat privately

Message any provider from the app. Phone numbers stay private until a booking is confirmed by both sides.

3

3. Book — instant or by quote

Book at the listed hourly/task rate, or request a custom quote for complex jobs. Every price is transparent.

4

4. Confirm & rate

Both sides mark the job complete. Payment happens directly. You can then rate the provider — reviews only count when the job is done.

Trust & safety

Built-in safety features.

🪪

ID verified

Providers submit government ID + selfie. Reviewed by our team before going live.

🔒

Private phone

Client and provider phone numbers never appear in public listings.

🚩

Report & flag

One tap to report a provider. Repeat issues auto-flag their profile for admin review.

📶

Offline schedule

Providers can view their booked schedule even without internet — great on job sites.
