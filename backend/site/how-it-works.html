FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an account to search?

No — you can browse every provider without signing up. You'll only need an account when you're ready to book or chat.

How do I know a provider is legit?

Each provider is ID-verified by our admin team. Look for the ✓ verified badge on their profile.

How is my phone number protected?

Phone numbers are hidden by default and only revealed to the other party once a booking has been confirmed.

What if a provider misbehaves?

Report them in-app; our team reviews every flag. Repeat offenders are auto-flagged and de-ranked or removed.
