Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data khedmaPro collects, how we use it, and the rights you have. It applies to the khedmaPro mobile app and website.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, role (client / provider), wilaya, and password hash.
  • Profile data (providers): service category, bio, hourly/task rate, portfolio images, ID documents for verification.
  • Booking data: requested services, addresses (only what you provide), messages, and reviews.
  • Device data: device model, OS, push token (when notifications are enabled), and (with permission) approximate location for radius search.

2. How we use your data

  • To operate the marketplace — matching clients with providers, sending messages, processing bookings.
  • To send transactional notifications (new booking, chat, review) via push and email.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and auto-flag providers who repeatedly underperform.
  • To improve the Service through aggregate, non-identifying analytics.

3. Phone number privacy

Phone numbers are never displayed on public profiles. They are only shared with the counterpart when a booking is confirmed, awaiting confirmation, or completed.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share the minimum necessary information with:

  • The other party in your booking (name, profile info, and phone only when unlocked).
  • Service providers we rely on to run the Service (hosting, SMS relay, push notifications, payments). All are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, when compelled by valid Algerian legal process.

5. Location data

The app requests your device location only to power the radius search. Location is used on-device to compute distances and is not stored on our servers unless you explicitly save a wilaya/coordinate to your profile.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data (Profile → Account).
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time.
  • Export your data by emailing privacy@khedmapro.dz.

7. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account we remove personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., billing records).

8. Security

We use TLS in transit, bcrypt for password hashing, and access controls on our infrastructure. No system is 100% secure — if we ever detect a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you promptly.

9. Children

khedmaPro is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. If we make material changes we'll notify you in-app or by email.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@khedmapro.dz.
